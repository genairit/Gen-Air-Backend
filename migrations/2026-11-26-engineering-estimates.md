# Engineering estimate rollout

Apply the companion SQL migration before deploying the Backend feature. The existing Worker must first support the authenticated engineering estimate PDF endpoint. No new environment variables are needed. Keep the existing Worker URL and service token.

Drafts are visible to their creator and estimate approvers. Engineers manage their own drafts. The engineering lead and system administrator may approve reviewed sketches. Quotation creation and export keep their existing separate permissions.

Source plan uploads and copies are private estimate attachments. The initial flow accepts PDF, PNG and JPEG plans up to 4 MiB. Marked sketch images are PNG or JPEG up to 512 KiB. Approval freezes the exact sketch bytes, area/image pairings, estimate inputs, and catalogue prices. Editing creates a new draft version. The prior reviewed revision remains in the database. The customer proposal requires approval in the existing quotation workflow and uses that quotation's commercial calculation.

The initial engine applies deterministic quantity rules to confirmed measurements. An explicit manual line for the same SKU in the same area supersedes all autogenerated quantities for that SKU in the area, so the engineer supplies the whole area-level quantity before repeats. The active catalogue SKU is unique. A missing SKU stays visible with a missing price. Installation-only retains measured installation and accessories and excludes manual equipment marked as indoor, outdoor or unit. Manual measurements still represent ventilation, floor heating and other specialist scope. Project drawings are not sent to an AI provider by this flow.
